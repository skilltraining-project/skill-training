Two screenplays were written from the same story. One is by a professional
screenwriter, the other by a model. Report where they differ as craft.

Work: {{work}}, episodes {{episode_range}}.

Both cover the same events, so plot differences are not interesting and you
should ignore them. What matters is everything the two writers did differently
with the same material.

## What to look at

For each of these, first describe what each screenplay actually does -- who
writes longer, who writes denser, who cuts sooner. Only then say which of them
works better here, and why:

- **Dialogue as speech.** Complete sentences or fragments? Interruptions,
  trailing off, people talking past each other?
- **What a line carries.** Information, subtext, feeling. Which writer packs
  more into fewer words?
- **Action lines.** Concrete and specific, or abstract and adjectival?
- **Pacing.** How often the scene heading changes, how fast information is
  released, how a scene is left.
- **Physical detail.** How props and environment are used, and how much of it
  there is.
- **Anything else that shows up.** The five above are a starting point, not a
  checklist to fill in.

Ignore layout. Margins, indentation, how scene headings are capitalised, where
the page breaks: none of that is craft, and reporting it would crowd out the
things that are. Compare what was written, not how it was typeset.

Do not assume you know which direction the gap runs. Report it as you find it:
where the model writes more than the human, say so; where it writes less, say
so. Both directions matter equally.

For each difference you find, get concrete:

- Quote the specific lines from both screenplays.
- Say what the human writer was doing that the model was not, or vice versa.
- Say what kind of gap it is: a convention the model does not know, a pacing
  instinct it lacks, or a habit it has that professionals do not.

## Output

A markdown report in English, sections as you see fit. Another agent reads this
to decide what to change in a pool of writing rules, so vague observations are
wasted. The more specific you are, the more precisely it can attribute the
difference.

Write the report only. Nothing else.
