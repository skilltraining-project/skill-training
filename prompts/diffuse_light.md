Rewrite the screenplay below as a chapter of prose fiction.

This is a corruption step, run at a low setting. Keep the story recognisable and
keep the texture, but remove enough that the chapter cannot be turned back into
the original screenplay by transcription.

Chapter number: {{episode_number}}

## Output format

First line: `# Chapter {{episode_number}}: <a short title, 3-6 words>`

Then 800-2000 words of prose.

Write everything in English, even if the screenplay below is in another language.

## Remove

- About half the dialogue. Turn it into what the character was trying to do. In
  the half you keep, use the exact words only where the plot depends on them,
  and report the rest indirectly.
- The denser stage business -- you do not need every expression, pause and
  gesture rendered in prose.

## Keep

- The main chain of events and the order the scenes happen in.
- Character names, relationships, genre furniture, reversals, key props.

## Never write

- Screenplay vocabulary: `INT.`, `EXT.`, `V.O.`, `O.S.`, `CUT TO`, `FADE IN`,
  `(CONT'D)`, `[VFX]`, scene numbers, revision marks.
- ALL-CAPS character names. `SARAH` in the screenplay is `Sarah` here.
- Parenthetical stage directions like `(coldly)`.
- Any note about what you did, or any mention of the source being a screenplay.

Output the chapter only.
